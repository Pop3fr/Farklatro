FARKLATRO Rules & Scoring

🎯 Core Push-Your-Luck Loop

Roll 6 dice each turn. Select scoring dice to add their Base Points to your Turn Pot. Choose Keep & Roll to lock those dice and roll the rest—each push adds +0.5x Mult! If all 6 dice score, you trigger HOT DICE! (+2.0x Mult and all 6 dice refresh!). If a roll has zero scoring dice, you FARKLE and lose your unbanked Turn Pot!

🎲 Special Dice Types

Gold Die: Grants +$2 if left unscored on the table when you Bank!
Glass Die: Multiplies Turn Mult by ×2 when scored, but has a 1-in-4 chance to crack back to Standard after banking.
Weighted Die: Heavily weighted toward rolling 1s and 6s.
Wild Die: Acts as a 1 for singles, or automatically morphs into whatever face completes a 3-of-a-Kind, Straight, or Three Pairs!
